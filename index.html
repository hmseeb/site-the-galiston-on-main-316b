Our Story

A Historic Downtown Gem, Reimagined

Tucked into a beautifully preserved, century-old building on Main Street, The Galiston on Main blends timeless character with thoughtful, modern updates. Following a full 2024 renovation, our 2,000-square-foot venue now offers a bright, versatile space featuring original brick walls, oversized windows, and a layout that adapts effortlessly to your event.

Whether you're planning an intimate wedding, a joyful baby shower, or a milestone birthday celebration, our team is here to make the details simple so you can focus on the moment. We're proud to be a trusted part of the Mesquite community — and we can't wait to host you.
